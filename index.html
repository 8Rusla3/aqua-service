Waterproofing

Exterior Foundation Waterproofing

The main waterproofing steps in a compact, clear overview.

🛠️

Process at a glance

A durable exterior system built to stop water, protect your foundation, and keep your home dry.

  • Locate utilities and protect the work area
  • Excavate to the weeping tile and prep foundation walls
  • Seal cracks and apply Aqua Block waterproofing
  • Install dimple board and protective poly-tarp membrane
  • Fit new weeping tile, filter cloth, and gravel drainage
  • Backfill cleanly, restore the area, and schedule follow-up

Special note: Work is based on normal soil and working conditions. Some fill space is required.
